# Soul of the Forgotten

A **narrative-driven 3D pixel art adventure**, where you embody a stranded Soul with no memory or name. Guided by a mysterious butterfly, you must explore sacred shrines, recover fragments of your past, and rebuild your humanity.

## 📖 Story
You awaken deep underground, voiceless and forgotten. A spectral butterfly leads you toward three legendary temples:
- 🌳 **Temple of the Forest** – where ancient roots guard hidden truths  
- 🌋 **Temple of the Volcano** – where fire-forged puzzles await  
- ❄️ **Temple of the Ice Mountain** – where storms and snow test your resolve  

Each shrine conceals memory fragments that reshape your form. At the final sanctuary, the demon **END** will rise to stop your rebirth.

## 🎮 Gameplay
- 3D pixel art exploration with dynamic effects  
- Environmental puzzles and intuitive challenges  
- **Metamorphosis system**: Soul → Humanoid → Shadow-Human → Complete Human  
- Narrative delivered through memory shards and animated murals  
- Immersive original soundtrack  

## ✨ Key Features
- Seamless visual and mechanical transformations tied to story progression  
- Rich art worlds inspired by classic RPGs and Metroidvania adventures  
- **Liri**, the poetic butterfly, offers hints and narrative insights  
- A focused solo prototype highlighting core mechanics  

## ⌨️ Controls
- **Movement**: `W` `A` `S` `D`  
- **Jump**: `Spacebar`  
- **Spells** (select with mouse wheel):  
  - `U` – Rapid spell (fireball)  
  - `I` – Mid spell  
  - `O` – Long spell  

## 🎨 Visual & Art Direction
- Pristine white landscapes, crystalline pillars, drifting snow particles  
- Hauntingly beautiful soundtrack with wind chimes, low strings, and blizzard ambience  
- Dynamic lighting and atmospheric transitions  
- Nonlinear progression: choose your area, some paths may aid others
